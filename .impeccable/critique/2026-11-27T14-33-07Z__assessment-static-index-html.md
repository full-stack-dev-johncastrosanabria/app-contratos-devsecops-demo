#### Anti-Patterns Verdict

Authored for this product and not interchangeable with others in its category. The 8-stage DevSecOps line runs down the first column of a semantic stage × delivery table. The rows have numbered station rings coloured by the worst state, DS/CO line bullets, and a "Núcleo determinista" interchange between stages 4 and 5. When all three deliveries agree on a stage, it collapses into one "Igual en v1, v2 y v3" cell that spans the columns. Each column head shows the test that decides REN-001 ("Prueba «cancelado no genera aviso»: fallida"). The inspector opens on the stage's "Pregunta de gobierno", and a final "Decisión humana" row closes each column. A generic SaaS console could not reuse any of this unchanged. Some generic residue remains. The white rounded panels on the ice-blue canvas, the bordered pill buttons and the dossier (disclosure rows plus a list of artifact links) read like a standard admin template. The interchange bar is mostly metaphor: it carries no data.

#### Overall Impression

Entry is calm and credible. The navy masthead, the rule in one sentence and the headline 'Dictamen REN-001: 2 de 3 entregas con brecha · 2 acciones esperan decisión humana · corridas en caché (0 tokens)' tell the visitor nothing is spending tokens, and the 0-token engine was picked for them with a reason. The peak comes early, at the three column heads: green v1 'aprobada', red v2 'fallida', amber v3 'Ninguna prueba vinculada'. A valley follows on scrolling. Past stage 4 the story muddies: a heavy navy bar with no data, amber dotted boxes in all three columns including the verified v1, mid-word truncation, and a 27.78% 5xx rate labelled 'Informativo'. A non-technical viewer feels doubt exactly where the product promises certainty. The high-stakes moment is well cushioned. The governance box explains idempotency before you commit, focuses Cancelar, and gives a 5 s Deshacer. One gap remains after the countdown: the panel goes quiet during the Boards call, the one moment that needs reassurance most. The ending is good. The result renders inline with the Boards reference link and takes focus, and the ledger's decision row updates, so the session closes on a confirmed, auditable outcome (peak-end positive).
